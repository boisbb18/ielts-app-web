Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my password?
On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and follow the email instructions to set a new one.
How do hearts work?
You start with a set number of hearts. You lose one for an incorrect answer, and they refill over time. You can always finish your current lesson.
How do streaks and daily goals work?
Complete lessons each day to build your streak and reach your daily goal. A reminder helps you stay consistent.
How do I sign in?
You can sign in with your email, with Apple, or with Google.
